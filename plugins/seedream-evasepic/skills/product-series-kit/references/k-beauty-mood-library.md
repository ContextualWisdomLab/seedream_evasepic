# K-Beauty Mood Library

Curated visual + tonal presets for Korean cosmetic / skincare video series. Each preset includes: color palette, lighting style, model archetype, wardrobe, setting, voice tone, and recommended camera approach.

Use as a starting point — mix and match based on product positioning.

---

## Preset 1: Clinical Minimalism (CERACLINIC / dermocosmetic vibe)

**Use for:** Sensitive skin, medical-adjacent brands, science-first positioning.

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Color palette** | `#FFFFFF` pure white, `#F8F4EF` ivory, `#D4C5B2` soft sand, `#2C3E50` deep slate (accent) |
| **Lighting** | Soft diffused overhead lightbox, even fill, no harsh shadows, cool-neutral color temp (5600K) |
| **Model archetype** | 30s Korean woman, confident and calm, scientist/expert aura |
| **Skin** | Natural texture, soft dewy finish, visible but cared-for pores, no makeup or minimal BB |
| **Hair** | Glossy dark brown, pulled back in a sleek low ponytail or loose bun |
| **Wardrobe** | Ivory silk shirt, off-white linen top, clinical-grade white coat (for lab scenes) |
| **Setting** | Minimalist bathroom with marble counter, clinical office corner with white ceramic dishes, white backdrop studio |
| **Voice tone** | Measured, expert, warm-but-precise. Short confident sentences. |
| **Recommended camera** | Static medium shot + slow dolly-in on product, tripod-locked |
| **Mood words** | `clinical`, `precise`, `restrained`, `trustworthy`, `quiet-luxury` |

**Example image-prompt anchor line:**
> "clean clinical minimalism with quiet-luxury warmth — ivory (#F8F4EF) and soft sand (#D4C5B2) dominate, soft overhead lightbox, no harsh shadows"

| Element | Spec |

| **Hair** | Warm dark brown, loose waves or messy low bun with tendrils |

| **Setting** | Cozy bedroom, kitchen with wooden counter, small balcony with plants |

## Preset 3: Editorial Premium (BAERE / luxury positioning)

**Use for:** Premium product launches, fragrance, anti-aging hero products, high-ticket SKU.

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Color palette** | `#1A1A1A` near-black, `#8B7355` aged gold, `#C9A876` champagne, `#3D3D3D` charcoal |
| **Lighting** | Single dramatic rim light from the side, low-key ambient, glowing product edge |
| **Model archetype** | 30s-40s Korean woman, refined, composed, high-fashion runway energy |
| **Skin** | Polished but natural, editorial retouch feel, strong cheekbone shadow |
| **Hair** | Sleek slicked-back, tight low bun, or editorial volume (hairspray defined) |
| **Wardrobe** | Black silk slip, ivory cashmere turtleneck, structured blazer, gold hoops |
| **Setting** | Dark-void studio, marble plinth, black velvet backdrop, minimalist gallery wall |
| **Voice tone** | Whispered, confident, sparse. Silence between words. Declarative statements. |
| **Recommended camera** | Slow orbit around product, dramatic tilt reveal, locked-off hero shot |
| **Mood words** | `editorial`, `premium`, `dramatic`, `refined`, `luminous` |

**Avoid:** "cinematic", "luxury" — use "editorial" and "refined" instead.

## How to pick a preset

Ask the user these questions (only if they haven't specified):

1. **Product category?** → Clinical (→ Preset 1), Natural (→ Preset 2), Premium (→ Preset 3), Mass-market (→ Preset 4), Wellness (→ Preset 5)
2. **Price point?** → Low (Preset 4) vs mid (Preset 1, 2) vs high (Preset 3, 5)
3. **Target age?** → Gen Z (Preset 4) vs Millennial (Preset 1, 2) vs 30s+ (Preset 3, 5)
4. **Trust vs delight?** → Trust-first (Preset 1, 3) vs delight-first (Preset 2, 4)

---

## Mix-and-match guidelines

- Use ONE preset as the anchor for the whole series (consistency)
- You CAN vary lighting slightly across episodes within the same preset (morning → midday → evening arc)
- You CANNOT mix Preset 1's clinical look with Preset 4's playful bright look in the same series — they're tonally opposed
