# Episode 3 — DEMO

**Purpose:** Physically show the product being used. Texture, application, sensory detail. This is the "proof-of-use" episode that builds desire.

**Emotional job:** "I want to feel that."

---

## Beat structure (15s)

| Time | Beat | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| 0-5s | Prep / approach | Model about to apply — dropper pressed, pump primed, product near skin |
| 5-10s | Application moment | Product meets skin (the hero ASMR-style moment) |
| 10-15s | Sensory finish | Absorbed / blended / finished — skin reaction visible |

---

## Image prompt guidance

**Shot type:** Extreme close-up or macro — skin and product together.

**Subject framing:** Hands + product + skin. No full face necessary. Focus on the sensory interaction.

**Composition:** Diagonal action lines (dropper descending, finger tracing). Skin takes up 40-60% of frame.

**Lighting:** Soft and flattering on skin. For cream/serum textures, slight side-light to catch shine/sheen.

**Mood anchor:** Tactile, intimate, calm.

---

## Video prompt guidance

**Camera movement:** `extreme close-up with gentle tilt down` OR `slow macro push-in` OR `static locked on skin`.

**Key action:** The single defining gesture — dropper squeezed, serum pooled, pressed into skin, blended with fingertips.

**Dialogue template (Korean):**
- (Silent or minimal) "이 텍스처 보세요. 바로 흡수돼요."
- "발리고 10초만에 사라져요."
- Or silent with breath sound only

**Dialogue template (English):**
- (Silent preferred) "Watch this. It just melts in."
- "No residue. Zero stickiness."
- Or silent with natural ambient sound

**Silent beat placement:** Beat 2 (5-10s) — the application moment should be SILENT. Let the visual and ambient audio (if `audioEnabled`) carry it. This is ASMR territory.

---

## Tone cues

- **Voice energy:** Almost whispered, reverent, unhurried
- **Pacing:** Very slow. Let every moment breathe. No rushing.
- **Speaker posture:** If face visible, soft gaze downward at hands

**Crucial for Seedance 2.0:** Use `slowly`, `gently`, `softly`, `tenderly` as degree adverbs. Without them the action looks jerky.

---

## What NOT to do in Episode 3

- ❌ Don't break the silence with a product pitch — let the demo speak
- ❌ Don't cut away from the application moment — hold it for at least 3 seconds
- ❌ Don't show the full bottle mid-application — we already know what it is
- ❌ Don't use fast motion or quick cuts — demo needs to feel tactile

---

## Texture-specific tips

| Product type | Demo focus |
|--------------|------------|
| **Serum / ampoule** | Dropper → single droplet → pressed into cheek with ring finger |
| **Cream** | Scoop from jar → dot on back of hand → blended across cheek |
| **Cleansing balm** | Scooped → warmed between fingers (melts visibly) → massaged on dry skin |
| **Mist** | Held 20cm from face → 2-second spray → droplets settle on skin |
| **Lip product** | Applicator on lip → single sweep → lip part with sheen visible |
| **Fragrance** | Spray into air → model walks through mist (with eyes closed) |
