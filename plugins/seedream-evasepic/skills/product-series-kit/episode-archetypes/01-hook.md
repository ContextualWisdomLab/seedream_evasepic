# Episode 1 — HOOK

**Purpose:** Grab attention in the first 2 seconds. Create a pattern-interrupt or curiosity gap that makes the viewer stop scrolling.

**Emotional job:** "Wait, what?" or "That's me."

---

## Beat structure (15s)

| Time | Beat | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| 0-3s | Attention grab | Bold visual, strong eye contact, or provocative question |
| 3-8s | Context | What's the problem being addressed? (subtle, not preachy) |
| 8-15s | Product tease | Product appears (not yet explained) + hook line delivered |

---

## Image prompt guidance

**Shot type:** Medium close-up — model's face visible, product held at chest height.

**Expression:** Direct eye contact with camera, slight knowing smile or raised eyebrow.

**Composition:** Rule-of-thirds, model offset slightly left, negative space right for label.

**Lighting:** Match brand preset. For hook, slightly brighter/cleaner than rest of series to signal "look here."

**Mood anchor:** Curious, direct, confident.

---

## Video prompt guidance

**Camera movement:** `slow dolly-in` or `static with subtle push` — pulls viewer IN.

**Key action:** Model looks at camera → (optional beat) → brings product into frame → delivers hook line.

**Dialogue template (Korean):**
- "혹시 {문제}인 분, 이거 보세요."
- "{클레임} 진짜예요?"
- "{연수}일 만에 바뀐 거 보세요."
- "이 제품 하나로 {결과}됐어요."

**Dialogue template (English):**
- "You need to see this."
- "If you've ever {problem}, this is for you."
- "In {N} days, everything changed."
- "This is the one."

**Silent beat placement:** Beat 2 (3-8s) — model holds product up, doesn't speak. Let the visual talk.

---

## Tone cues

- **Voice energy:** Direct, confident, slightly surprised tone (as if sharing something just discovered)
- **Pacing:** Unhurried. Let the opening look land before speaking.
- **Speaker posture:** Leaning slightly toward camera, intimate but composed

---

## What NOT to do in Episode 1

- ❌ Don't explain features yet — that's Episode 2's job
- ❌ Don't show texture / application — that's Episode 3
- ❌ Don't cram dialogue (hook works best with 1 short line + 1 silent beat)
- ❌ Don't start with product-only (viewer needs a face to connect)

---

## Example fill (CERACLINIC series)

**Episode 1 title:** "속당김의 진실"

**Image brief:**
- Model holding ampoule at chest height, direct gaze, slight curious half-smile
- Minimalist bathroom, soft diffused lightbox
- Composition: rule-of-thirds, label visible right side

**Video brief:**
- [00:00] Model looks directly at camera, calm confidence.
- [00:04] She brings the ampoule up slowly from out of frame, tilts it to catch the light. (silent beat)
- [00:09] She speaks: "속당김, 진짜 원인 뭔지 아세요?" Slight pause. Gentle smile.
- Camera: slow dolly-in throughout, tripod-locked, medium close-up.
