# Episode 4 — TESTIMONIAL

**Purpose:** Social/experiential proof. Model (or quoted real user) describes the result or transformation. This is where emotion lands.

**Emotional job:** "She's like me. If it worked for her, it could work for me."

---

## Beat structure (15s)

| Time | Beat | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| 0-5s | Before-moment setup | Reference the problem state (wardrobe/setting subtle shift from Ep1) |
| 5-10s | The transformation claim | Spoken result — what changed and over what timeframe |
| 10-15s | Emotional payoff | Soft smile, subtle gesture, confidence — the vibe of "yeah, it worked" |

---

## Image prompt guidance

**Shot type:** Medium shot or medium close-up — face and upper body.

**Subject framing:** Model in more casual/candid pose than Ep1. Slight wardrobe evolution (relaxed, confident).

**Composition:** Rule-of-thirds, natural gaze off-camera (as if mid-thought) OR gentle direct gaze.

**Lighting:** Warmer than Ep1 — golden hour vibe OR soft window light. Feels more personal.

**Mood anchor:** Warm, confident, relatable.

---

## Video prompt guidance

**Camera movement:** `handheld medium shot, natural slight shake` OR `gentle pan as she shifts` — more human than tripod-static.

**Key action:** Model speaks to camera naturally — gesture with hands, slight head tilt, soft laugh or smile.

**Dialogue template (Korean):**
- "{N}주 쓰고 진짜 바뀌었어요. {구체적 결과}."
- "처음엔 반신반의했는데… 이제 다른 제품 못 써요."
- "아침에 거울 보는 게 달라졌어요."

**Dialogue template (English):**
- "It's been {N} weeks and honestly — {specific result}."
- "I was skeptical, but now I can't go back."
- "I look at myself differently now."

**Silent beat placement:** Beat 3 (10-15s) — a soft knowing smile, subtle head nod, or slight laugh. No words. Let the emotional payoff land.

---

## Tone cues

- **Voice energy:** Warm, genuine, slightly vulnerable. Uses natural filler ("진짜", "솔직히", "honestly").
- **Pacing:** Conversational rhythm — pauses, half-sentences, a laugh mid-line.
- **Speaker posture:** Relaxed, one hand occasionally touching face or hair. Human, not poised.

**Crucial:** Avoid "acted" perfection. The testimonial fails if it feels scripted.

---

## What NOT to do in Episode 4

- ❌ Don't repeat the benefit claim word-for-word from Ep2 — tell it through personal result
- ❌ Don't over-describe the product itself — this episode is about the model's experience
- ❌ Don't use the exact same lighting/wardrobe as Ep1 — signal that time has passed
- ❌ Don't hit the CTA here — that's Episode 5

---

## Before / after suggestion

If the brand wants a visual before/after, this is the episode for it:

- **Option A (split screen):** Left side = Day 1 (cooler, slightly fatigued skin), Right side = Day {N} (warmer, dewy, alert). Risky — Seedream sometimes struggles with split composition.
- **Option B (sequential):** Describe the "after" state only, let the dialogue reference "before" verbally. Usually more reliable output.

Default to Option B unless user specifically requests split-screen.

**Video brief:**
- [00:00] Medium shot of her in morning bedroom light, looking off to the side as if mid-thought. She speaks: "2주 쓰고 나서 제일 달라진 거요?"
- [00:06] She turns back to the camera, soft smile growing. She speaks: "아침에 거울 보는 시간이 짧아졌어요. 속당김이 없어서."
