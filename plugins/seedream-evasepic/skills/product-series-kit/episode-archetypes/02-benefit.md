# Episode 2 — BENEFIT

**Purpose:** Deliver the core "why this matters" message. Answer the curiosity opened in Episode 1 with a clear benefit statement backed by one visual proof point.

**Emotional job:** "Oh — that's what makes it different."

---

## Beat structure (15s)

| Time | Beat | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| 0-5s | Context statement | Name the problem or need (short, empathetic) |
| 5-10s | Benefit reveal | Key feature/ingredient/mechanism — SHOW, not just tell |
| 10-15s | Credibility anchor | Visual proof (ingredient macro, lab shot, wordmark highlight) |

---

## Image prompt guidance

**Shot type:** Close-up or extreme close-up — emphasize product detail, label, or ingredient.

**Subject framing:** Can be model-less (product hero) OR model's hand presenting product with extreme focus.

**Composition:** Product centered or golden-ratio off-center, deep macro focus on the hero element.

**Lighting:** Directional — emphasize texture/label. For clinical brands, cleaner. For premium, dramatic side rim.

**Mood anchor:** Educational, authoritative, visually striking.

---

## Video prompt guidance

**Camera movement:** `slow tilt down` OR `gentle push-in on product` OR `static with subtle zoom`.

**Key action:** Camera/focus travels from wider frame to the specific proof element. Label appears crisp. Ingredient name called out.

**Dialogue template (Korean):**
- "핵심은 {성분}. {효능}이에요."
- "이 {성분}이 {메커니즘}해줘요."
- "{N}%의 {효과}, 임상으로 입증됐어요."

**Dialogue template (English):**
- "The secret is {ingredient}. It {mechanism}."
- "Clinically proven {stat}."
- "{N} weeks, {result}."

**Silent beat placement:** Beat 3 (10-15s) — camera lingers on the label / ingredient name. Let the credibility visual land silently.

---

## Tone cues

- **Voice energy:** Measured, expert. Softer volume than Episode 1.
- **Pacing:** Slower. One benefit, one pause, one proof.
- **Speaker posture:** Composed, slight forward lean during the benefit reveal

---

## What NOT to do in Episode 2

- ❌ Don't stack 3+ benefits (pick ONE hero claim)
- ❌ Don't skip the visual proof — telling without showing loses trust
- ❌ Don't repeat the hook line from Episode 1
- ❌ Don't introduce the demo yet — that's Episode 3

---

## Example fill (CERACLINIC series)

**Episode 2 title:** "세라마이드 5종의 비밀"

**Image brief:**
- Extreme close-up of the ampoule label with "5 CERAMIDES" wordmark centered
- Dropper emerging from the bottle, single golden serum droplet catching light
- Clinical soft white lighting, ivory/gold palette

**Video brief:**
- [00:00] Camera opens on the model's hand holding the ampoule at eye level. Label faces camera. Medium close-up.
- [00:05] Slow zoom in on the label, "5 CERAMIDES" comes into sharp focus. She speaks: "핵심은 세라마이드 5종. 피부 장벽 그대로 복원해요."
- [00:11] Extreme close-up of the dropper, a single serum droplet hangs. Silent beat — droplet slowly falls out of frame.
- Camera: slow push-in transitioning to macro focus pull, tripod-locked, clinical diffused lighting throughout.
