# Episode 5 — CTA (Call to Action)

**Purpose:** Wrap the series with a memorable, action-driving finale. The viewer has seen the hook, benefit, demo, and testimonial — now give them the frictionless next step.

**Emotional job:** "I want this. Where?"

---

## Beat structure (15s)

| Time | Beat | Intent |
|------|------|--------|
| 0-5s | Call-back | A visual or verbal echo of Ep1's hook (closes the loop) |
| 5-10s | Product hero | Final clean product shot — the "memorable last image" |
| 10-15s | Action line | Spoken CTA with clear instruction (link, store, event) |

---

## Image prompt guidance

**Shot type:** Medium shot with product prominent, OR pure product hero wide shot with brand context.

**Subject framing:** Option A — Model holding product at chest height, direct gaze, confident. Option B — Product-only hero on clean surface with brand palette dominant.

**Composition:** Centered or rule-of-thirds, with clear negative space for on-screen text (CTA text overlays added in post).

**Lighting:** Match Ep1 closest — this is the bookend. Brand-signature lighting at its cleanest.

**Mood anchor:** Confident, conclusive, aspirational.

---

## Video prompt guidance

**Camera movement:** `slow pull-back from close-up to medium` OR `static hero shot` — gives the viewer a sense of conclusion.

**Key action:** Final gesture — model holds product up, subtle nod, then stillness. OR product-only with subtle environmental motion (wind, mist, light shift).

**Dialogue template (Korean):**
- "{제품명}, 지금 {채널}에서 만나보세요."
- "링크 프로필에 있어요."
- "{할인/이벤트} 지금이에요."
- "써보면 아실 거예요."

**Dialogue template (English):**
- "{Product}. Link in bio."
- "Try it yourself. {Channel}."
- "You'll know once you try it."
- "Available now at {store}."

**Silent beat placement:** Beat 2 (5-10s) — a clean silent product shot with brand signature lighting. Let the visual be the final brand impression.

---

## Tone cues

- **Voice energy:** Confident, clean close. No hard-sell urgency.
- **Pacing:** Unhurried. End with a breath, not a rush.
- **Speaker posture:** Composed, slight final smile, direct eye contact on CTA line

**Crucial:** Don't panic-close. A calm confident CTA outperforms an excited one for K-beauty.

---

## On-screen text considerations

Seedance 2.0 struggles with rendering text mid-video. Best practice:

- **Keep spoken CTA as dialogue** — model delivers it verbally
- **Add on-screen text in POST** (CapCut, Premiere) — don't ask Seedance to render "LINK IN BIO" text
- If you MUST include text in the video prompt, put it in quotes: `The brand wordmark "CERACLINIC" visible on the label`

---

## What NOT to do in Episode 5

- ❌ Don't add new product information — the selling is done
- ❌ Don't cram CTA + testimonial + benefit — pick ONE action
- ❌ Don't use different model/wardrobe than Ep4 (break in continuity kills the payoff)
- ❌ Don't end on silence alone — CTA must be SAID or clearly implied
- ❌ Don't use hard-sell adjectives ("BUY NOW!", "LIMITED!") — off-brand for K-beauty premium

---

## CTA variants by arc

| Series arc | Best CTA style |
|------------|---------------|
| **Hook-Benefit-Demo-Test-CTA (default)** | "Link in bio" or "Shop now at {store}" |
| **Skincare routine** | "Full routine in my bio" |
| **Makeup tutorial** | "Products tagged below" |
| **Premium launch** | "{Product} launches {date}. Waitlist open." |
| **Ritual/Spa** | Soft suggestion: "Try it. You'll feel it." |
