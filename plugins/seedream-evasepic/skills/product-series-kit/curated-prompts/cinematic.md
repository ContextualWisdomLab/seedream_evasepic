# Curated Prompts — Editorial / Dramatic (NOT "cinematic" — forbidden word)

High-production-value prompt patterns curated from community Seedance 2.0 usage. Use as starting-point references when the user wants an elevated, editorial look.

**Note:** "cinematic" is a forbidden word on Seedance 2.0. We call this category "editorial" or "dramatic" instead.

---

## Pattern 1: Dark-void premium reveal

Use for: luxury product hero, fragrance, premium ampoule/serum.

```
A frosted glass ampoule bottle with matte gold dropper cap stands on a black
marble plinth in a dark-void studio. A single warm rim light from the left edges
the bottle's curve with a soft golden glow, the label faintly illuminated. The
background fades to pure black at the frame edges.

[00:00] Slow orbit camera begins from the right, the rim light sliding across the
bottle's glass surface, catching the golden cap reflection.

[00:07] Camera reaches the front, product fully lit along one edge, label reads
crisp and clean against the dark void.

[00:12] Camera slows to a stop, holds steady on the hero frame, rim light gently
pulsing.

Camera: slow orbit, gimbal-smooth, close-up framing throughout. Editorial
lighting, single warm side rim light against a true black backdrop, dramatic
high-contrast restraint.

The product must remain visually unchanged — same bottle shape, same label, same
cap. No additional props. No text overlays. Steady motion, no warping. The rim
light direction stays constant from first to last frame.
```

**Key moves:**
- Dark void = pure black background, no gradient
- Single warm side rim light (never front-lit)
- Slow orbit or static (never handheld)
- ZERO dialogue — silent product hero

## Pattern 2: Haute couture fashion drama

Use for: bold launch reel, statement piece, fragrance campaign.

```
An editorial portrait: a composed Korean woman in a black silk slip stands in a
minimalist gallery space, a single pool of amber light from above casting a
sharp shadow at her feet. Her hair is slicked back, her gaze is distant and
serene.

[00:00] Medium shot. She holds @(img1) at chest height — the ampoule bottle
catches the amber light, glass glowing warm against her dark silhouette.

[00:06] Slow tilt down follows her hand as she lowers the bottle to a black
marble plinth beside her. Her gaze doesn't follow — she stares ahead.

[00:12] Tilt returns to her face. A long quiet beat. She speaks: "침묵 속에서
만들어진다."

Camera: slow tilt down and back up, static framing, medium shot throughout.
Editorial dramatic single overhead amber light, near-black backdrop, refined
restraint.

The product from @(img1) must remain visually unchanged. Model's face, hair,
and wardrobe stay constant. Steady motion, no warping. Black marble surface
details remain crisp.
```

**Key moves:**
- Single overhead hero light
- Distant/serene gaze (not smiling)
- Sparse whispered dialogue — one line max
- Editorial composure

## Pattern 3: Warm golden hour ritual

Use for: natural beauty, wellness, warmth-forward brands.

```
A 30s Korean woman in an oversized cream knit sits on a linen-draped bed by
a window. Late afternoon golden light pours in from behind her, edging her hair
warm and soft. A ceramic tray of amber skincare bottles rests on the bedside.

[00:00] Medium shot. She slowly reaches for @(img1) (frosted glass ampoule) on
the tray, her hand moving gently through the golden backlight.

[00:06] Close-up of her hand lifting the bottle, the light catching the glass
curve, a soft amber highlight moving across the label.

[00:11] Back to medium shot. She holds the bottle close to her chest, softly
closes her eyes for one breath. A small contented smile. (silent beat)

Camera: static medium shot → slow push-in to close-up → return to medium. Warm
golden-hour backlight from the window throughout, amber fill on skin.
Documentary, warm and quiet.

The product from @(img1) must remain visually unchanged. Model's skin retains
natural texture — light freckles, visible pores, soft dewy finish. Steady
motion, no warping, no exaggerated hair blow.
```

**Key moves:**
- Golden hour backlight = non-negotiable
- Slow reach / close eyes / breath moments
- Silent beats outnumber spoken ones
- Documentary style anchor

## Usage with product-series-kit

These patterns are starting points. For each of the 5 episodes, adapt the pattern's structure but:
- Change the beat intent to match the archetype (hook/benefit/demo/testimonial/cta)
- Keep the LIGHTING and CAMERA signature consistent across all 5 episodes
- Vary shot type (close / medium / wide) per episode
