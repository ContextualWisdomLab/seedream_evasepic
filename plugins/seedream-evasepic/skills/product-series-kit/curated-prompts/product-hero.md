# Curated Prompts — Product Hero (No Person)

Pure product-focused prompts with elemental effects — water splash, mist, glowing edge, floating texture. No model, no hands. The product is the entire story.

Use for: hero product reveals, luxury launches, texture showcases, ingredient campaigns.

---

## Pattern 1: Clinical Splash

Use for: serums, ampoules, hydrating essences.

```
A frosted glass 30ml ampoule bottle with matte gold dropper cap stands centered
on a polished white ceramic surface. Label reads "CERACLINIC" in crisp serif.
Background is a soft gradient from ivory (#F8F4EF) at the bottom to pure white
at the top. Soft diffused overhead lightbox fills evenly.

[00:00] Static establishing shot, product centered, label facing camera. A
single translucent water droplet forms mid-air above the bottle's cap.

[00:04] The droplet slowly falls, slow-motion. It meets the cap with a gentle
silent splash, water rippling outward in soft concentric waves along the cap
surface.

[00:10] The water settles into a thin shimmering sheen along the bottle's
shoulder, catching the overhead light. Camera holds steady.

Camera: static, tripod-locked, medium close-up throughout. Soft diffused
overhead lightbox, clinical even fill, cool-neutral 5600K, gentle reflections.

Photorealistic, restrained and clean. The product must remain visually
unchanged — same frosted glass bottle, same label, same gold dropper cap. Water
effect is subtle and photographic, not fantasy. No distortion, no warping.
```

## Pattern 2: Golden Mist

Use for: fragrances, mists, luxury oils.

```
A tall slender fragrance bottle with amber-tinted glass and a brushed gold cap
stands on a black marble plinth. A single warm practical lamp casts a pool of
golden light from the upper-left, the bottle's silhouette sharp against a near-
black backdrop.

[00:00] Static shot, product centered, light pool concentrated on the bottle's
right side. Fine golden mist begins drifting in from the right edge of the frame,
slowly curling around the bottle.

[00:07] The mist wraps the bottle gently, illuminated by the warm practical,
amber particles visible in the beam. The bottle's label glows faintly through
the haze.

[00:12] Mist thins, the bottle emerges fully lit, warm reflection along the
gold cap. Frame holds steady on the hero beat.

Camera: static, tripod-locked, medium shot with gentle negative space above.
Editorial dramatic single-source warm practical light against true-black
backdrop, atmospheric mist catching the beam.

Photorealistic, editorial, refined. Product remains visually unchanged — same
bottle geometry, same label detail, same cap finish. Mist moves slowly and
naturally, no VFX cartoonishness. No watermarks, no overlays.
```

## Pattern 3: Floating Texture Macro

Use for: cream/balm products, texture hero shots.

```
Extreme macro close-up of a cream texture — a generous dollop of pearlescent
white-gold cream pools on a matte porcelain surface. The cream's surface has a
slight shimmer from a low-angle key light. The frame is entirely filled with the
cream texture and the porcelain surface.

[00:00] Static macro, cream pooled, slight specular highlight on its curved
surface. A ceramic spoon enters slowly from the right edge.

[00:05] The spoon gently lifts a portion of the cream, creating a soft peak.
The texture stretches before separating — visible creamy strands catch the light.

[00:10] The spoon lifts out of frame, leaving the base dollop and a thin
glossy trail. Camera holds steady on the remaining texture.

Camera: static macro, tripod-locked, extreme close-up throughout. Low-angle
warm key light creating dimensional specular highlights on the cream's curved
surface. Soft diffused fill.

Photorealistic, editorial macro. The cream texture is rich and natural — no
artificial glow or sparkle effects. The porcelain surface remains clean and
matte. No hand visible, only the ceramic spoon. Steady precise motion.
```

## Pattern 4: Ingredient in Nature

Use for: botanical-ingredient stories, natural-origin positioning.

```
Extreme macro close-up of a single fresh calendula flower petal resting on
damp moss, backlit by morning sun. Water droplets cling to the petal's edge,
catching the amber light. A single drop of golden oil slowly rolls across the
petal's surface.

[00:00] Static macro of the petal, sun from behind edging it golden. A single
golden oil droplet forms at the petal's top, held in tension.

[00:05] The droplet slowly releases and rolls down the petal's central vein,
the amber sunlight catching it as it descends. The oil's trail glistens.

[00:11] The droplet settles at the petal's tip, hangs briefly, then falls out
of frame onto the moss below. Petal returns to stillness.

Camera: static macro, tripod-locked, extreme close-up. Warm morning backlight
from the upper-right, natural dappled shadow. Documentary botanical.

Photorealistic natural macro. The petal and moss texture remain precise and
detailed. Oil droplet moves with realistic surface tension. No VFX shimmer,
no artificial color grade.
```

## When to use no-person prompts

Product-hero (no person) works best for:
- **Episode 1 (Hook)** of a premium-launch arc — dark void establishing mystery
- **Episode 2 (Benefit)** of any arc — ingredient macro story
- **Episode 3 (Demo)** texture moments — extreme macro of product texture
- **Episode 5 (CTA)** premium close — final hero shot after model in Ep4

Avoid for UGC, testimonial, or bright-clean mass-market series — those need human presence.

---

## Common failure points

- **Label text garbled**: Use quoted text in prompt: `label reads "BRANDNAME"` — Seedream honors quotes better
- **Product geometry drifts**: Add `"must remain visually unchanged"` as final constraint
- **Splash/mist looks cartoonish**: Use `"photorealistic"`, `"subtle"`, `"natural surface tension"` — avoid `"explosive"`, `"magical"`
- **Background bleeds in**: Specify `"backdrop stays true black"` or `"pure white seamless"`
