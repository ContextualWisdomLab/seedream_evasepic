# Curated Prompts — K-Drama Romantic Style

Cinematic-adjacent (we call it "editorial") prompts inspired by K-drama aesthetics. Useful for fragrance, premium anti-aging, or campaign films where emotion carries the message.

**Forbidden word reminder:** Don't use "cinematic" — use `editorial`, `film-style`, `dramatic`, `restrained`.

---

## Pattern 1: Classroom warmth (Japanese/Korean school drama)

Use for: Gen-Z / youth-targeted brands, first love campaigns, coming-of-age positioning.

```
A warm afternoon in an empty classroom. Golden sunlight spills through slatted
blinds onto side-by-side wooden desks, fine dust motes floating slowly in the
light beams. A young Korean woman in a summer school uniform sits at a desk,
writing in a notebook, long black hair and stray strands gently lifted by a
soft breeze through the window. Her long eyelashes cast subtle shadows on her
cheeks.

[00:00] Extreme slow push-in from medium shot of the desktop to a close-up of
her side profile, focused on her writing. Skin is naturally pink, subtle smile
in concentration. Breathing is light and even.

[00:05] She pauses, pen lifted. She slowly turns her head toward the window,
her gaze distant. The afternoon light catches a hint of moisture in her eyes.

[00:11] A soft smile grows — small, private, almost embarrassed. She returns to
her writing. Camera gently pulls back to the medium shot.

Camera: extreme slow push-in → static hold → gentle pull-back. Shallow depth
of field, creamy blurred background, warm film grain.

Film-style Japanese-Korean drama aesthetic. Character face, hair, and uniform
remain consistent throughout. Real subtle head tilts and eye movements. No
text overlays, no subtitles, no watermarks. Light direction stays constant —
warm golden afternoon backlight from the window side.
```

**Key moves:**
- Golden afternoon backlight through blinds
- Extreme push-in to close-up
- Subtle emotion — half-smiles, turned head, gentle breath
- Shallow DOF with creamy blur

## Pattern 2: Rainy cafe contemplation

Use for: warm premium skincare, fragrance, journaling/mindfulness positioning.

```
A quiet cafe corner on a rainy afternoon. Rain streaks down the window beside
a small wooden table. A 30s Korean woman in a beige oversized sweater sits
with a ceramic cup, her gaze on the rain outside. A frosted glass ampoule
bottle rests on the table beside her notebook.

[00:00] Medium shot, her profile visible, rain-streaked window dominates the
right side of the frame. She slowly wraps both hands around the warm ceramic
cup.

[00:06] She turns her head slightly toward @(img1) (the ampoule bottle) on the
table. Her gaze softens. She picks it up gently, holding it with quiet
reverence.

[00:12] Close-up on her hand holding the bottle, the rainy window blurred
behind creating a bokeh of warm yellow cafe lights and cool blue rain streaks.
She speaks softly: "이 하나로 충분해요."

Camera: static medium profile shot → slow push-in to close-up of hand and
bottle. Warm ambient cafe lighting mixed with cool rainy-window backlight,
bokeh of warm and cool tones.

Film-style documentary contemplative. Shallow depth of field, natural grain.
The product from @(img1) remains visually unchanged. Rain motion is gentle and
continuous, no sudden drops. Steady frame, no shake.
```

**Key moves:**
- Rain-window backlight creating bokeh
- Warm/cool color contrast (cafe light vs window)
- Whispered single-line dialogue
- Slow push-in to hand-and-product close-up

## Pattern 3: Empty subway / emotional solo

Use for: premium launch, independent-woman positioning, night-time products.

```
A 30s Korean woman stands alone on an empty subway platform at night. Cool
white fluorescent lights overhead, amber pools from old ceiling fixtures cast
warmer spots on the platform floor. She wears a long black coat, holds a small
leather bag. An ampoule bottle peeks out from her coat pocket.

[00:00] Wide establishing shot, she stands at the edge of the platform, gaze
down the tunnel. Fluorescent cold above, amber pool at her feet. Empty. Still.

[00:06] Slow push-in to medium shot. She turns to face camera slowly, her gaze
distant but composed. A train's distant rumble vibrates the air.

[00:12] She speaks, softly, almost to herself: "혼자 있는 시간이 제일
강해요." The corner of her mouth lifts, half a smile. She turns back toward
the tunnel.

Camera: static wide → slow push-in to medium shot → static hold. Mixed cool
fluorescent + warm amber practical lighting, high contrast against dark
backdrop.

Dramatic film-style, editorial restraint. Character face, hair, and wardrobe
consistent. Real subtle head movements. Train rumble is distant and ambient.
No overlays, no watermarks.
```

**Key moves:**
- Cool/warm mixed lighting (subway fluorescents + amber practicals)
- Wide → push-in → hold structure
- Single confessional line of dialogue
- Empty space = emotional weight

## When to use K-drama style for a 5-ep series

K-drama style works best for:
- **Premium launch arc** — sustain the mood across all 5 eps
- **Fragrance** — emotion-first, product secondary
- **Anti-aging campaigns** targeting 30s-40s demographic

Don't use for: bright UGC, mass-market youth brands (too slow), clinical/dermocosmetic (too emotional).

---

## Cross-episode continuity for K-drama style

Maintain across all 5 episodes:
- Same model, same signature wardrobe (dark or cream)
- Lighting DNA: ONE warm light source + environmental cool mix
- Dialogue: 1 short confessional line per episode, always soft volume
- Silence carries ~40%+ of each 15s clip
- Camera: slow movements only, no handheld, no quick cuts
